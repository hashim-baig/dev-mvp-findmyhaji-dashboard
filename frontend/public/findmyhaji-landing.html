What is FindMyHaji?

Connecting hearts and souls across distances during the most sacred journey of a lifetime.

Bridging Distance with Technology

FindMyHaji was born from a simple yet profound need – keeping families connected during Hajj and Umrah. When millions gather in the holy cities, staying in touch with loved ones becomes both crucial and challenging.

Our platform serves three communities: pilgrims seeking guidance and safety, families yearning for updates from their loved ones, and service providers managing groups with care and responsibility.

Through real-time location sharing, emergency alerts, ritual guidance, and seamless communication, we ensure that the spiritual focus remains undisturbed while practical concerns are addressed.
